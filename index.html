Reality Check: The Solo Portfolio Gap

Employers can often tell when projects like LankaLawBot or GlobalBooks were group assignments where you handled one slice (like UI design). You need a Solo Full-Stack Proof of Competence proving you can wire a database (PostgreSQL) to a backend (Node.js) to a frontend (Next.js) entirely on your own. This perfectly aligns with the highest local job market demand and bridges your current UI/UX skills into full-stack engineering.

Routine: Wed-Sun Deep Work (6-8 hrs). Mon-Tue Light Work (2 hrs).
